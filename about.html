MY TEACHING INTERESTS

Explaining concepts
through practical examples

My coursework uses examples from computer science, particularly Java programming and algorithms. These examples help me consider how a complex topic can be divided into smaller parts and connected to students’ previous knowledge. They also provide a context for examining how students apply what they have learned.

In my teaching philosophy, I explain that my interest in Java developed through playing mobile games. This example shows why I consider practical relevance important when introducing a subject. If students can understand where a concept is used, it can give them a reason to learn more about it.

I believe that clear explanations and opportunities to ask questions are important parts of teaching. In this portfolio, I examine how these beliefs can be connected to learning activities and assessment. The course designs are proposed applications for future teaching and do not represent courses I have already taught.
