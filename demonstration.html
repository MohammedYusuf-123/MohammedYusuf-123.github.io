Why didn’t Maya learn?

Understanding student learning through one student’s journey. An AHE 603 presentation by Mohammed Yusuf Mujawar.

THE QUESTION

In the case presented, Maya scores 88% on a test. However, two weeks later she is unable to apply the same concept in a different situation.

This raises a question about what evidence is needed to determine whether she has understood the concept.

THE LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Diagnose one common barrier to student learning and select one teaching move that could address it.

The presentation begins with a case and a quick vote. It then uses a diagnosis question to connect learning theories to possible teaching responses.

Purpose of the demonstration

The demonstration examines the difference between answering a familiar question and applying the same concept in a new context. It introduces prior knowledge, cognitive load, and transfer as possible barriers to learning. Each barrier is then connected to a teaching method that could help address the problem.

For example, a question about previous knowledge can help identify what students already understand. Dividing an explanation into smaller parts can help manage unfamiliar information. Providing examples in different contexts can also help students consider where the same principle applies. These methods address different learning problems, so the choice should depend on the difficulty identified.
