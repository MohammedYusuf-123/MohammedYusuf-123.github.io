Activity 10.1: Lecture Style Inventory Scoring: Should show a total number for each category so learners can match their totals to their styles. Fascinator: 49 Fascinator: The Fascinator hopes to share important information and ideas related to the topic, including key concepts and interesting details. This type of lecturer will try to find the best organizational method for conveying the information so learners will best understand it. The Fascinator has studied the topic for a long time and wants to find the best way to share this knowledge with learners. Inspirer: 23 Inspirer: The Inspirer wants to convey and instill passion for the topic. This type of lecturer shares the essence of the subject with the learners but wants them to connect with it on another level. Inspirers will often use a story or several stories from their own experiences, or from others’ experiences, to convey important points. This type of lecturer wants to inspire confidence and instill inspiration that can make students want to learn. Energizer: 6 Energizer: The Energizer often feels energized when lecturing and in return tries to share that energy with learners. This type of lecturer wants learners to be excited not only by the content of the lecture but also by the presentation of it. The Energizer seeks to build excitement and enthusiasm among learners. The Energizer will likely tell a few jokes and use fun activities to encourage learner involvement.
Report page 15 of 16
Performer: 22 Performer: While Performers have information or processes to convey, they at least partly see their job as engaging and entertaining learners. This type of lecturer will think through the lecture from start to finish ahead of time, including the theater of it, such as where they will stand, how they will use gestures, and what points they will emphasize verbally or visually. Performers want learners to view them as professional and prepared to present. They may well see themselves as a part and parcel of the presentation and may have a flare for the dramatic.
