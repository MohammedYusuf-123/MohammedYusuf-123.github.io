Syllabus review

What does this instructor seem to value? 

The instructor values their student to learn concepts on coding and algorithms where they are able to write, design, and implement various algorithms. Instructor also values clear communication with students. Instructor check the complete performance of the students before assigning any grades.

What will students actually spend their time doing? 

Students spend their time on using the Canvas Learning Management system to learn the interactive textbook, class materials, completing assignments, taking quizzes and practice the coding in the IDE of their choice.

What kind of student behavior does the grading system reward? 

Instructor awards overall understanding and problem-solving ability of the students. The optimized and simple solutions are also more appreciated as that is one of the goals of the course.

How well do the learning goals/objectives/outcomes map to the assessments that measure them (from what you can tell)? 

Not enough information on the assignment details and what topics are covered.

What questions or anxieties would you have if you were a student in this class?

No topic content for the course and pre-requisite to prepare for, and the deadline are too strict. And the fear of getting 0 if I miss any assignment.

What are the key sections included on the syllabi? 

Outcomes, Class information, communication plan, course web site details, critical code of conduct, Textbooks and software details. Attendance policy, and grading policy. Tutoring policy.
