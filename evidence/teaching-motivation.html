The Instructor Motivation Quiz is an informal assessment with questions that help you identify your motivation for teaching. Considering what incentivizes us as instructors can provide us with a glimpse into why we make the choices we do and may help us be more effective in implementing strategies that motivate students.

Category Descriptions Connection: 5 This motivation style is characterized by a strong desire to be a part of a group or community. People with this style feel happiest and most fulfilled when they're surrounded by others, whether in a small gathering or a large social movement. Collaboration and teamwork are highly valued, and solitary pursuits may feel isolating or unfulfilling. Security: 0 This motivation style is focused on stability and structure. People with this style want to feel secure in their professional lives and prefer the structure of an organization. They value predictability and prefer to know what to expect regarding income, job responsibilities, and future prospects. This style is often associated with a desire for financial stability and the security of having a regular income. Influence: 30 This motivation style is focused on leadership and inspiring others. People with this style enjoy imagining and bringing new ideas to life,

and they have a natural ability to lead and motivate others to act in positive ways. They thrive on the process of getting others to follow their vision, and they're often seen as natural-born leaders. Accomplishment: 0 This motivation style is driven by a desire for recognition and measurable success. People with this style enjoy activities that involve a clear "win" or achievement. They may turn anything into a competition and enjoy the feeling of being the best at what they do. They thrive on external validation and recognition for their achievements. Enlightenment: 15 This motivation style is focused on a deeper sense of purpose and meaning. People with this style may feel less motivated by external rewards like wealth or fame and instead seek a greater understanding of their place in the world. They may contemplate their role and responsibilities in a larger cosmic context and may be driven by a desire for personal growth and self-discovery Note: This is an informal assessment intended simply to provide you with a starting point for reflection.
