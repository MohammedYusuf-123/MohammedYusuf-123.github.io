Teaching Philosophy statement

I believe students learn best when they are interested in the subject or have fun while studying. If we are successfully able to make the topic interesting and worth to learn then I believe students will learn the best.

Example – I was interested in learning Java since I loved playing games on mobile phones.

As an educator my role is make sure the student are understanding the concepts clearly and why we are learning them and answer any questions they have. If I can give real life or practical applications, it is easier for them to learn.

Example – If the students can see the practical implications of the concepts, then it is easier to understand for them. For example, how wind energy can be used to generate electricity.

One way I connect course content to student’s prior knowledge or experience is by giving

example for our day-to-day life or some common event occurred where they are able to relate to the topic.

Example – If I am teaching about the combustion engine then I can take inspiration from cars.

I know that meaningful learning has happened when the students can understand, interact and question about the topic. If they are critically thinking and questioning about the topic I believe they are learning.

Example – Even when a small question is asked and answered then there is a chance for everyone to learn from.

I will continue to develop my teaching through examples and making sure my student are confident in the topic and never afraid to ask questions.

Mohammed Yusuf Mujawar.
