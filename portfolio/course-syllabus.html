Purpose of the activity

A course syllabus brings together the main decisions involved in planning a course. In this activity, I developed a proposed CS 110A course with a description, learning outcomes, assessment methods, policies, and a tentative schedule. The design provides a way to examine how these parts relate to one another.

FROM THE COURSEWORK

Designing an Introductory CS Syllabus

The proposed course combines explanations, live coding, guided practice, discussion, and low-stakes checks. The assessment weights are programming assignments 35%, labs and practice 20%, quizzes 10%, midterm examination 15%, and final project 20%.

02 / REFLECTION ON THE ACTIVITY

Reflection

The syllabus allows me to consider whether the activities and assessments support the approach described in my teaching philosophy. For example, programming exercises provide practical applications, while discussion and concept checks create opportunities to ask questions. However, including these activities does not by itself show that the course is well aligned. Each outcome still needs appropriate practice and a suitable way to assess it. The proposed workload and sequence also need to be examined from the perspective of students who have no prior programming experience.

03 / PROPOSED TEACHING APPLICATION

Future teaching application

I would connect each learning outcome to the exercises and assessments that address it. Before a graded assignment, I would provide practice and feedback on the concepts students need to use. The proposed course policies would also need to be checked against the requirements of the institution where the course is offered.
