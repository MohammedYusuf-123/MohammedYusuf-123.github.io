Purpose of the activity

The Lecture Style Inventory describes different preferences an instructor may have when presenting content. The activity helps me examine how I prepare explanations, engage students, and reflect on a lecture. It also provides a basis for considering methods that could complement my preferred approach.

02 / REFLECTION ON THE ACTIVITY

Reflection

The higher Fascinator score is related to my interest in explaining a subject clearly and making its concepts understandable. However, a clear and interesting lecture does not necessarily show that students can apply the information. My presentation about Maya addresses this issue by asking whether a strong test score is enough evidence of learning. This helps me consider why a lecture should include opportunities for students to explain their reasoning and use the concept, along with the explanation provided by the instructor.

03 / PROPOSED TEACHING APPLICATION

Future teaching application

I would organize a short explanation around one main concept and include a prediction or application question before moving on. If students have difficulty explaining their answers, I can revisit the concept or use another example. The recording of my demonstration can provide a basis for examining how these opportunities were handled once it is available.

AREA FOR FURTHER DEVELOPMENT

I would examine the evidence of student understanding during a lecture and use the recording to identify where pacing or explanation could be improved.
