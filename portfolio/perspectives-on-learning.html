My Perspectives on Learning

Purpose of the activity

Different theories of learning explain different aspects of how students develop knowledge and skills. This inventory considers the teaching choices associated with four perspectives. The purpose of the activity is to examine my preferences and consider how they may influence the learning activities I choose.

02 / REFLECTION ON THE ACTIVITY

Reflection

The higher score for cognitivism is related to my interest in how students process information and understand an explanation. My PCK response and class plan also consider the pace and amount of information presented. However, one theory may not explain every learning situation. Students may also need opportunities to work with an idea, discuss it with others, and relate it to their own experiences. Therefore, I would use the inventory to examine my preferences while remaining open to other approaches that are appropriate for the task.

03 / PROPOSED TEACHING APPLICATION

Future teaching application

For a new programming concept, I would begin by asking students what they already know about the topic. I would then introduce a small example and ask them to work through a related problem. Their explanations would help me understand how they are connecting the new information to their existing knowledge.

AREA FOR FURTHER DEVELOPMENT

I would select a teaching method according to the learning problem and examine students’ responses, rather than relying only on my inventory result.
