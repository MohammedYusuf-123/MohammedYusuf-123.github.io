What Motivates Me to Teach

This activity examines what motivates me to teach and how these motivations may influence my teaching decisions.

Purpose of the activity

The Instructor Motivation Quiz considers different factors that can make teaching meaningful to an instructor. Understanding these factors can help me examine why I prefer certain approaches and how those preferences relate to student learning. The results are intended for reflection rather than a fixed classification.

02 / REFLECTION ON THE ACTIVITY

Reflection

The results suggest that making a useful contribution to students’ learning and developing my understanding of teaching are important motivations for me. However, having a strong intention to help students does not establish whether the teaching method is effective. I would also need to consider what students understand and what they can do after the activity. Similarly, a lower score in another category does not mean that student relationships or professional stability have no value. The results help me identify priorities, but they should be considered along with the actual teaching situation.

03 / PROPOSED TEACHING APPLICATION

Future teaching application

I would explain why a learning activity is being used and ask students how they could apply the concept in another course or a practical problem. This can help me relate my intention to make teaching useful to the way students understand the purpose of the activity.

AREA FOR FURTHER DEVELOPMENT

I would consider students’ explanations and application of the concept along with my own satisfaction when evaluating a teaching activity.
