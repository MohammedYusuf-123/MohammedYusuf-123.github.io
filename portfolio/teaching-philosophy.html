Purpose of the activity

A teaching philosophy explains the beliefs that guide an instructor’s decisions. In this activity, I describe how interest in a subject, connections to everyday life, and opportunities to ask questions can support learning. The statement also helps me examine whether my proposed teaching methods are consistent with these beliefs.

02 / REFLECTION ON THE ACTIVITY

Reflection

I believe that students are more willing to learn a topic when they understand why it is useful. However, interest alone does not show that they have understood the concept. For example, a student may enjoy a programming demonstration but still be unable to explain why the program produces a particular result. My presentation on student learning discusses a similar issue through Maya’s difficulty applying a concept in a different situation. This connection helps me consider how I can check understanding after developing students’ interest in the topic.

03 / PROPOSED TEACHING APPLICATION

Future teaching application

I would introduce a concept using an application that students can relate to, and then explain the main idea behind it. After this, I would ask them to explain the concept in their own words or apply it to another problem. Their responses would help me identify which parts require further explanation.

AREA FOR FURTHER DEVELOPMENT

I would connect each belief in my teaching philosophy to a specific classroom activity and identify how that activity could provide evidence of student understanding.
