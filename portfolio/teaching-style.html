Purpose of the activity

The Engaged Teaching Inventory describes different roles an instructor can use when teaching. These include explaining subject content, demonstrating a process, facilitating learning, and supporting independent work. This activity helps me consider which roles I prefer and where I may need to adjust my approach according to the needs of the students.

02 / REFLECTION ON THE ACTIVITY

Reflection

The results suggest that I prefer to guide students while also providing explanations and demonstrations. This is related to my teaching philosophy, where I discuss the importance of answering questions and making concepts clear. However, students may require different amounts of guidance depending on their previous knowledge. If I explain every step, they may have fewer opportunities to develop their own reasoning. If I provide too little support, they may struggle to understand how to begin. This means that I need to consider both the difficulty of the task and the support students require.

03 / PROPOSED TEACHING APPLICATION

Future teaching application

For example, when introducing a programming problem, I would first explain a short solution and show how the code works. I would then give students a related problem and ask them to explain their approach. If they are unable to proceed, I can provide a smaller hint or another example. This would help me adjust the support without completing the whole task for them.

AREA FOR FURTHER DEVELOPMENT

I would use a short question after an explanation to check whether students are ready to work independently or need additional guidance.
