Sources and portfolio information

This section identifies the course materials and explains how the original activities are presented alongside the portfolio commentary.

Course context

AHE 603: College and University Teaching, Fall 2026, taught by Dr. Claire Major at The University of Alabama. The supplied Fall 2026 v2 syllabus informs the course-objective connections. The full syllabus is not republished here.

Course reading

Barkley, E. F., & Major, C. H. (2026). Engaged Teaching: A Handbook for College Faculty. Social Good. The supplied chapter collection supports the course themes; chapter PDFs are not redistributed.

The presentation cites Chapter 3, Understanding Student Learning, and The K. Patricia Cross Academy. Bibliographic wording in the original PowerPoint is preserved in its slide text.

Inventory materials

The K. Patricia Cross Academy inventories supplied with the coursework include the Engaged Teaching Inventory, You Might Be a Constructivist If Inventory, Instructor Motivation Quiz, and Lecture Style Inventory. Displayed scores are transcribed from the supplied reports. These informal self-assessments are used for reflection, not as evidence of teaching effectiveness.

How I would use these teaching resources

The course readings and inventory reports provide starting points for developing my teaching. I would use the chapter on pedagogical content knowledge to examine how I explain a programming concept, the student-learning chapter to consider prior knowledge and transfer, and the lecturing chapter to review the structure of a presentation. The inventories provide prompts for reflecting on my preferences, but they do not establish that a method is effective. Comparing these resources with other teaching guidance is an area I can develop further.

Original work and portfolio commentary

Original evidence is linked from each activity. The ten activities have readable HTML transcriptions, with wording preserved and layout adapted. PDF reports also retain a link to the original report where selection formatting may be clearer. The proposed course syllabus is a design exercise rather than an official institutional document.

The portfolio commentary connects the original work with my teaching approach and areas for further development. Future applications are proposals rather than claims of completed teaching or measured student outcomes.

Images and media

The portrait was supplied by the author. Presentation previews are rendered from the supplied PowerPoint. The recording has not yet been added. No stock photography or external tracking is used.

Portfolio scope
